## 2. Sanity check vs. draft `interjection_appropriate` (NOT gold, Tier 1 only)

Same caveat as `focus_sanity_check.md`: `interjection_appropriate` in the
evaluation set is my own quick Phase 1 judgment, not an independent
annotation. Agreement: 12/19 (63.2%) dev, 13/16 (81.2%) test — unchanged
by eval-set scaling since this comparison is restricted to Tier 1.

Looking at the disagreements, two clear, explainable patterns emerged —
investigated, not tuned away:

- **"Would suggest ANOTHER interjection" vs. "would an interjection fit
  here at all"** are different questions. E.g. "Oau, așa arată ploaia?"
  and "Vai, Chris, sunt gata de mult timp!" were drafted
  `interjection_appropriate=True` (an interjection does fit) but the
  system predicts `False`, because one is *already present* — the system
  is correctly answering "should I suggest adding one," and correctly
  declines to suggest a second (objectives.md: "prevent repeated
  interjections"). The draft field never distinguished these two
  questions.
- **Emotion-detector conservatism cascades here too**: "Ce panică!" and
  "Este trist ce se întâmplă-n lume." were drafted as interjection-
  appropriate, but "panică" and "trist" both hit the Phase 6 tied-category
  case (equal NRC weight across two emotions — see
  `emotion_baseline_report.md`), so emotion resolves to `unspecified` and
  the confidence gate blocks any suggestion. Consistent, traceable
  behavior — not a new defect in this phase.
- **The rule engine is more liberal on mildly-positive informational
  text** than my own quick draft judgment was — e.g. "Antrenamentele
  cognitive sunt extrem de utile, dar pot fi și foarte distractive."
  triggered a `happy`-confidence suggestion the draft had marked
  inappropriate. Plausibly the draft was the more conservative/correct
  call here; not resolved, flagged for the eventual real annotation pass.

## Decision

Formal-text safety (the actual acceptance criterion) is solid. The softer
disagreement rate reflects known, explained gaps in the *comparison data*
(the draft field conflates two different questions, and inherits Phase 6's
conservatism) as much as in the system — not chased further, per the same
reasoning as `focus_sanity_check.md`.
