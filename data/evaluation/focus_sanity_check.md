# Phase 8 focus detector — sanity check (not a gold evaluation)

Generated by `objective_evaluation.evaluate_focus` against `data/evaluation/{dev,test}.jsonl`'s
`focus_words` field.

**This is not a gold-standard evaluation.** Unlike `emotion_baseline_report.md`
(REDv2/hria human labels), `focus_words` in the evaluation set is
`provenance="predicted"` — my own quick draft judgment from the Phase 1
pilot about which word(s) seemed most salient in each sentence, not an
independent annotation with any defined methodology. A mismatch below
doesn't mean the rule engine is wrong; it may equally mean the draft was
the imprecise one.

## Results

| split | n (single-sentence) | precision | recall | F1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| dev | 19 | 0.500 | 0.065 | 0.114 |
| test | 16 | 0.500 | 0.043 | 0.080 |

## What this actually shows

Precision is moderate (when the rule engine does flag something, it's
usually also in the draft list) but recall is very low — the two mostly
disagree. Looking at the misses, this is explained by a real conceptual
mismatch, not a bug:

- **The rule engine targets *emphasis*** (objectives.md Phase 8's actual
  definition): capitalization, intensifiers, repetition, emotion-bearing
  words, contrastive corrections. Most of `data/evaluation`'s sentences
  (formal/news RONEC text, neutral hria lines) simply don't contain any of
  these signals, so the engine correctly stays silent.
- **The Phase 1 draft annotations were closer to *topical salience*** —
  "what's the sentence about" (e.g. "termenul" in "Termenul de livrare...",
  "intrusul" in a burglary story, "Consiliul Superior al Magistraturii" as
  the subject of a legal sentence) — a related but genuinely different
  notion from prosodic emphasis. Those drafts were written quickly during
  the Phase 1 pilot without a defined methodology for *this* distinction.

## Decision

Not tuning the rule engine to chase these numbers — that would mean
fitting to my own possibly-mistaken quick annotations rather than to
objectives.md's actual definition of focus. Recommendation for the future
300-sentence evaluation-set pass: re-annotate `focus_words` with emphasis
specifically in mind (or drop the field and treat topical salience as a
separate, future concept), so a real comparison becomes possible.
